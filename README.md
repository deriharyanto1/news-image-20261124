A one-page newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a
single image: **50 stories from 10 regions**.

The top story is fourteen nations recognising the ICJ ruling that Myanmar
committed genocide against the Rohingya. Nine panels cover Libya, the Strait
of Hormuz, NATO, the COP31 collapse, tech, the Ukraine war, Asia, markets
and sport, followed by a quote from UN chief Antonio Guterres and 39
one-line briefs from North America, South America, Western Europe, Eastern
Europe, the Middle East, Africa, South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia and
Oceania.

Everything is drawn programmatically by `main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only, no
network, no dependencies): a hand-written PNG encoder and a hand-drawn
vector stroke font render the whole page into `entry.png` (1024x1024).
